The translation method from German to Mandarin Chinese is not as ideal as supposed at least for two reasons:

1. Chinese allows massive adjunct cluster similar to German. Because of this, there is no lingustic limit to the length of a sentence. Also no restrction of statements allowed in the sentence.

ex. [Der Mann [dem der Nacht-club gehört] hat gesagt]: [der Geheim-dienst [von den Vereinigte Staaten] hat ihn gewarnt].
    -statement count: 4
ex. [[夜总会]老板说]：[美国[特勤局警告过他]]。
    -statement count: 4

2. Subordinate clauses in Chinese are extremtly productive, also similar to German. This means complex German senteces, when translated to Chinese, the complexity is not necessarily reduced. The sub-clauses stay as sub-clauses only in a different position. Usually not as stand-alone simple matrix sentences.

ex. Der [Geheim-dienst hat gesagt] [es gibt einen [Anschlag] auf den Nacht-club]
    -statement count: 3
ex. [特勤局称], [夜总会[有一場袭击]]。
    -statement count: 3

ex. [Angela Merkel hat gesagt], [[dass der Anschlag böse und schrecklich gewesen ist]].
    -statement count: 3
ex. [安格拉-默克尔说]，这次[[袭击是邪恶和可怕的]]。
    -statement count: 3
